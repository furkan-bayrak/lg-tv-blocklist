# Code of Conduct

We welcome participants of all backgrounds, identities, and experience levels.
Disagree with ideas, not people; explain your reasoning, credit others' work,
respect boundaries, and protect personal information. Harassment,
discrimination, personal attacks, threats, and sharing someone's private
information without their consent are not acceptable.

## Scope

This policy applies in project spaces, including issues, pull requests, and
comments, and when someone officially represents the project elsewhere.

## Reporting and enforcement

For abuse on GitHub, follow [GitHub's reporting instructions](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam).
Use **Report content** from the item's menu. If GitHub offers **Report to
repository admins**, you can send the report to the maintainers; otherwise,
report platform abuse to GitHub Support. Keep sensitive details out of public
issues and comments.

Maintainers may issue warnings, hide or remove content, or restrict
participation temporarily or permanently, depending on the severity and
recurrence of the conduct.

## Attribution

This policy draws on the principles of [Contributor Covenant 3.0](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/3/0/).
